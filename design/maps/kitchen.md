# Kitchen map (`kitchen`)

**Theme:** a paper plane folded from a recipe card falls down a giant cartoon kitchen: the top shelf, the countertop, the stove, the sink, then inside the fridge. Food and kitchen tools only.

**Verdict (v130, at launch):** built to avoid the mistakes the other maps showed.
- No obstacle species also appears in the background.
- Every look follows a part of the kitchen.
- Nothing pulls the plane, hides obstacles or makes the plane soggy.
- Original designs only.

The weakest fits are marked ⚠️ below.

## Spawn chances (v130)
These are the chances that a new row is each obstacle. I measured them by running the game's own spawner 40,000 times at each depth.
- Every obstacle has the same weight: about 1 in 19 (5%).

## Gaps and ideas
- **Push Zone:** a visible source would make it more fun, such as a little electric fan on the counter. It would need a hit body like Paper's desk fan.
- **More fridge looks:** the fridge layer has the fewest looks. Ideas: a jelly wobbling (swarm), a frozen fish stick (slider), a carton of eggs (bulge).
- **A cat:** you could add one later, but Paper already has the cat paw.
