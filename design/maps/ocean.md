# Ocean map (`sea`)

**Theme:** sinking through the sea, from the bright surface to the dark deep.

**Verdict:** the theme is strong and every obstacle belongs underwater. But this is the **thinnest map in the game**: 12 obstacles, one look each, and 8 of the 20 mechanics unused. Some of those unused mechanics have perfect sea versions, so this is the cheapest map to make more fun.

Fit: ✅ fits well · ⚠️ works, but something is off · ❌ doesn't fit

## Gaps and ideas (biggest opportunity in the game)
The map has no Wall Bulge, Bouncing Slider, Rising Projectile, Conveyor, Spinning Blades, Sweeping Arm or Spring Line. Natural sea versions:
- **Conveyor:** a school of fish crossing. This is the most natural fit for a conveyor anywhere in the game.
- **Rising Projectile:** a pufferfish puffing upward, or a rising air pocket.
- **Wall Bulge:** a big rock or brain coral bulging from the wall; deeper down, a sleeping whale's side.
- **Bouncing Slider:** a swordfish or a piece of driftwood.
- **Spring Line:** a fishing line between two floats. It bounces you, and the floats or hooks hurt.
- **Sweeping Arm:** a ship's propeller deep down, or an octopus arm.
- **Spinning Blades:** two crab claws snipping.

The map also needs **more looks per obstacle**, like the newer maps have (for example urchins / anemones / stonefish).
