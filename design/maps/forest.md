# Forest map (`forest`)

**Theme:** falling through a forest as the seasons pass, from spring to winter to a moonlit night. Nature only, no magic.

**Verdict:** one of the best-themed maps. Animals and plants act the way they would in a cartoon forest, and the looks change with the seasons. That makes the fall feel like a journey. Two obstacles break the "nature only" idea or simply don't make sense as dangers: the **sunbeam** and the **frosty ray**.

## Gaps and ideas
- The map already uses all its mechanics. The main fix is replacing the **sunbeam / frosty ray**.
- The **night layer** could get its own looks: fireflies (as a swarm), glowing mushrooms (static), owl swoops (a sweep).
